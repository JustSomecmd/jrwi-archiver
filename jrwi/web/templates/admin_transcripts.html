What the figures mean

Words found: the share of the transcript's words found, in order, in what the speech model heard. The lines' times rest on these, so it's how well the two agree; under 30% means the transcript isn't this episode's, and it isn't timed. It doesn't check the times themselves: on test episodes with a known answer, 90% of lines started within 0.7 seconds of it.

Lines placed: lines that got a time from words of their own. The rest sit right after the line before them, or, next to a long gap, show untimed.

Hearing: how long hearing the episode took, added up over pauses, without loading the model. Speed is the episode's length over that: 6× is an hour of sound in 10 minutes.

Memory: the most the speech model's process used. Threads lists each number it ran with, when the setting changed while it ran.
