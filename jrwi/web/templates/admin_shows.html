Shows

Posts are sorted the way the site sorts them: a show's own tag puts a post in its Episodes, "<show> Extras" in Extras and "<show> Rolled" in Just Rolled With It. A post with several of these is in each. Tags with the show's initials count too, such as "TMK Extras". Posts whose tags don't say are sorted by their title, so other names added here (comma separated) help those. For a single post, open Admin at the bottom of its page.
